Why a PWA Beats a Normal App

Same experience, none of the friction. Here's what you skip when you install DateWizza.

🔄

No Updates, Ever

PWAs update the moment you open them. No "Update Available" prompts, no downloads, no waiting. You always have the latest version.

📦

Minimal Storage

A PWA is measured in kilobytes, not megabytes. Most PWAs are roughly 300× smaller than a comparable Android/iOS apps.

🚪

No App Store

No account required. No reviews, no fees, no waiting for approval. You install directly from your browser — because it is the browser.

📡

Works Offline

Key parts of a PWA app can launch without a network connection. Perfect for mobile data, flights, or weak signal areas.

🚀

Instant Launch

Opens in standalone mode — no address bar, no tabs, no browser UI. One tap from your home screen and you're in.

🔒

Privacy-First

Audits run in your browser. No data is sent to our servers for processing — the same privacy model as the web version.

🍎

Install on iPhone or iPad

Apple doesn't allow a one-tap install from Safari. The steps below take about 15 seconds.

  1. 1

    Open datewizza.com in Safari

    Safari only — Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot install PWAs.

  2. 2

    Tap the Share button

    It looks like a square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, top-right on iPad.

  3. 3

    Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"

    If you don't see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the share sheet and enable it there.

  4. 4

    Tap Add

    The DateWizza icon appears on your home screen. Launch it — it opens in app mode, without the Safari UI.

On iOS 18+, you'll see an "Open as Web App" toggle in the install dialog. Turning it on gives you the cleanest app-like experience.

How to Remove the App

No hard feelings. Removing a PWA leaves no residue — the icon disappears and the space is freed.

  1. 1

    Long-press the DateWizza icon on your home screen until the icons start jiggling.

  2. 2

    Tap the × in the top-left corner of the icon.

  3. 3

    Tap Delete to confirm.

On iOS, removing the home-screen icon deletes the PWA. There is no separate data to clear.

  1. 1

    Long-press the DateWizza icon on your home screen or app drawer.

  2. 2

    Tap App info, then tap Uninstall.

  3. 3

    Confirm to remove. You can also find it in Settings → Apps → DateWizza → Uninstall.

  1. 1

    Chrome: open chrome://apps, right-click the DateWizza icon, choose Remove from Chrome.

  2. 2

    Edge: open edge://apps, click Details on DateWizza, then Uninstall.

Install FAQ

Do I have to install from an app store? ↓
No. DateWizza is a Progressive Web App — it installs directly from your browser in seconds. No app store account, no review process, no multi-megabyte download.
Where do I install it on Android? ↓
Open datewizza.com in Chrome on Android and either tap the Install App button at the top of this page, or open the Chrome menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. The icon lands in your app drawer just like any other app.
Do I have to update the app manually? ↓
No. PWAs update automatically. Every time you open DateWizza, you get the latest version. No "Update Available" notifications, no downloads, no waiting.
How much space does it use? ↓
Roughly the size of a couple of photos. A typical PWA is measured in kilobytes — DateWizza is around 200KB, which is 300× smaller than a comparable native Android app and 500× smaller than a native iOS app.
How do I remove it if something goes wrong? ↓
Long-press the icon on iOS or Android and delete it. On desktop, go to chrome://apps and remove it. Nothing is left behind — no hidden data, no folders, no residue.
Will the PWA and the website share my login? ↓
Yes. The installed PWA is the same DateWizza under the hood. Your login, your Pro status, your audit history — all shared automatically.
