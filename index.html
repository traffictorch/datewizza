FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is a "trip merge"?

When you have multiple errands or dates in a day, DateWizza groups nearby trips into one optimised route. Instead of three separate car journeys, you make one — cutting fuel, time and CO₂.

How are my savings calculated?

We use DEFRA and EPA emission factors per travel mode, combined with your actual trip lengths. Cash savings use average fuel/transit costs for your region.

Do my guests need an account?

No. Guests get SMS and email reminders plus a one-tap RSVP link. They can optionally create an account later to see their own savings.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — one click in billing. You keep Pro features until the period ends, then drop to Free. Your data stays put.

Is my data private?

Your events live in your account only. Public events are opt-in. We never sell data, and Stripe handles all payment details end-to-end.
